# ShowKey privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026. This policy covers the ShowKey Chrome extension published by Kaprrrr Studios.

## Live audio

When you click **Start live audio**, Chrome sends sound from the selected browser tab to ShowKey.
ShowKey changes its pitch on your computer and sends the result to your audio output.
Audio passes through temporary buffers in memory.
ShowKey does not record, save, upload, or share that audio.
Click **Stop live audio**, or close the tab, to end processing.

## Song information and saved settings

Opening ShowKey gives it access to the active tab’s title and URL.
It uses that information to identify the current recording and show any saved key.
While live audio is active, ShowKey checks the selected tab for song changes.

When you click **Save song**, ShowKey stores the title, song link, recording key if selected, and pitch shift in this Chrome profile.
Saved entries stay on the device until you remove them using **×** in the setlist or uninstall the extension.
ShowKey does not sync the setlist, send it to the publisher, sell it, or share it with advertisers.
ShowKey has no account, analytics, or tracking service.

## Music websites

When you open a song link, your browser visits that music website.
That site handles your sign-in and playback under its own privacy policy.
ShowKey does not receive your music account credentials.

## Limited use and contact

ShowKey uses information from Chrome APIs solely to transpose audio in the tab you select and remember the songs you save,
in accordance with the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data), including its Limited Use requirements.

For a privacy question, contact the publisher through [ShowKey support](https://github.com/Kaprrrr/ShowKey/issues).
GitHub issues are public.
